# Parity checklist: Editing and canvas interactions

*Source: independent read of Blue Grid v0.87 (read-only). Tools, selection, wiring, zones, subsystems, shortcuts, files, persistence, touch, accessibility.*

Decision key: **KEEP** carry over; **REDESIGN** keep the purpose, change the design; **MERGE** fold into the named target; **DROP** do not carry over; **LATER** keep, in the named phase.

The **Owner** column is for you: put ✓ to accept the proposed decision, ✗ to reject it, or a note.

*Owner column filled on 2026-11-27: the owner chose to follow the Elephant's recommendation on every row (✓ accept as proposed, ✓ → X change to X). Two rows (UI-001, UI-002) were marked by the owner directly and match. 25 rows were the owner's call and were taken by delegation, not by review.*
Items marked *unverified* were not confirmed by running or fully reading the code.
